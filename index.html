How much can a double-decker bus lean before tipping over?

In real-world conditions, a double-decker bus typically reaches rollover risk at around 25-35° of body lean, depending on speed, load, and centre of gravity.

How Long does Yutong E10/12 last on a single charge?

A Yutong E10 or E12 can typically travel up to 275 miles (443 km) on a single charge, depending on passenger load, weather, and driving conditions.

Who owns the destination boards?

The destination board on the front of a bus is braned by Hanover on some of the fleets and Mobitec on other fleets.
